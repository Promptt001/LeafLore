Villager Holocrons New

Capture any villager's knowledge into an Empty Holocron (an echo shard) and imprint it onto another villager later — profession, type, level, XP, name & trades included. Records live in records.yml and survive restarts.

  1. Mine or fish — Empty Holocrons drop from almost every ore (deepslate & nether varieties, even ancient debris) and can be fished up.
  2. Right-click a villager with an Empty Holocron to record its profession, type, level, XP, custom name and visible trades.
  3. Right-click another villager with the Charged Holocron to overwrite it with the stored data.
