The world

Now running on LeafLore

LeafClaims

A golden shovel still marks out your land, but everything else happens in a menu. LeafClaims is the claims plugin we run here: claim, trust your friends, name your base, and open it up to visitors when you are ready.

  • One menu for everything. Claim, trust, permissions and claim blocks all live in /claimgui, so you are not memorising commands.
  • Your friends on your terms. Give people Build or Manage trust on a claim, and use the public permissions screen to choose what any visitor may do.
  • Claim warps. Give your base a name such as spawn_market and let people /visit it. Landings are checked for lava, fire and other traps before every visit.
  • Claim blocks. Claim blocks are how much land you may claim. See your balance in the menu, buy more if the server has an economy plugin set up, and gift blocks to a friend.

How the server is set up

The settings that change how the world plays, and the commands you are most likely to want on day one.

World settings

  • Seed: 1563068244
  • Spawn radius: 100 blocks
  • Sleep: 10% of players in a bed skips the night
  • Phantoms: enabled, but they will not attack until you turn them on with /togglephantoms
  • Ender pearls do not despawn on death
  • Villagers in a bucket: you can pick a villager up and carry it

Rules & notes

  • No spamming. Chat abuse leads to a mute.
  • No abusive language. Repeated toxicity has consequences; warnings come first.
  • No lag machines. Utility mods are fine, but do not use them to hurt server performance.
  • Respect claims. If it is not yours and you were not trusted, leave it alone.
